# 01 · Visual Identity

## One-sentence vibe

> **A quiet, dark-first money instrument — precision-engineered hairlines, tabular numerics, and a single electric-blue accent doing all the lifting.**

## What it feels like

Linear's surgical density meeting Things 3's calm restraint: an app that looks at home in a homelab terminal next to `btop`, but polished enough to put in a portfolio. The emotional register is **serious, calm, and confident** — never austere, never decorative. Numbers are the protagonists; chrome is the stage hand.

## Reference brands

| Brand | What we're borrowing |
|---|---|
| **Linear** | Density, command bar, keyboard-first instincts |
| **Things 3** | List rhythm, restraint, careful whitespace |
| **Stripe Dashboard** | Numeric hierarchy, ledger discipline |
| **Wise** | Multi-currency cues, FX honesty |

## Register

**Aim for:** calm · serious · dense · precise · quiet

**Avoid:** playful · decorative · airy · gradient-heavy · illustration-heavy

## Brand mark

The logo is a **half-circle gauge arc** with a dot beneath it — a direct reference to the "Income used" meter on the dashboard. Rendered in primary blue.

Sizes available: 16px (favicon), 24px (sidebar header), 64px+ (hero / login).

## Brand colour story

- **Primary brand → blue** (`220 95% 66%` dark / `220 90% 56%` light). Used for active state, primary CTAs, focus rings, the logo, branded surfaces.
- **Income → green** (semantic). Stays green everywhere it means "money in."
- **Expense → red-orange** (semantic). Negative deltas, money out.

The brand colour and the semantic colours are intentionally separate. Never use primary blue to mean "positive." Never use income green to mean "this button is the main action."
